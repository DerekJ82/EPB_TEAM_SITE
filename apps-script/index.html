Calendar · Meeting Cadence

Recurring ceremonies.

The standing meetings that keep EPB S&P aligned week to week and month to month.

Weekly

  1. MON

    EPB S&P Stand-Up

    15-minute team sync. Blockers, priorities, and anything cross-functional that needs a fast decision.

  2. THU

    Pipeline Review

    30-minute review of PSO, CBR, and BR Queries pipeline status. Flags any at-risk deliverables before the weekend close.

Monthly

  1. WK 1

    EPB Monthly Business Review

    2-hour deep-dive on results vs. plan. Includes revenue, margin, and pipeline health across all LOBs.

  2. WK 2

    Consolidated BR Signoff

    Final sign-off on the Consolidated Billed Revenue workbook before distribution to business partners.

  3. WK 3

    Flash Preparation

    Flash Weekly Report and Newsletter prep session. Aligns messaging and confirms data sources before publication.

Quarterly

  1. Q+1

    Quarterly Planning Review (QPR)

    Full-day planning session aligning strategy, resourcing, and roadmap for the coming quarter. Key deliverable: updated EPB S&P priority stack.

  2. Q+2

    StratCheck

    Mid-quarter strategy check. Reviews progress against QPR commitments and flags any pivots needed.
